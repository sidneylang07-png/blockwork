Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Who is Blockwork for?

Neighbours looking for local help and independent service providers who want to be discoverable in their community.

Can I search by postal code?

Yes. Enter a full postal code or the first three characters, and the site can prioritize nearby providers.

Does Blockwork handle payments?

In this starter version, providers and customers arrange payment directly. You can later integrate Stripe or another payment provider.

What services can be listed?

Any legitimate local service: dog walking, babysitting, tutoring, cleaning, haircuts, photography, repairs, music lessons, lawn care, and more.
